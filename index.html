Research

I'm interested in the intersection of Robotics and Machine Learning. My research focuses on learning world models of manipulation skill effects: predicting how objects move under contact-rich skills such as push, pick, and place from partial point clouds, capturing the multimodal outcomes that contact creates, and using these models for multi-step planning on real robots.
