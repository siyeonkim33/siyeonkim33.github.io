Research

My research focuses on robot learning for contact-rich manipulation, with an emphasis on world models and planning under uncertainty. To manipulate everyday objects, a robot must anticipate what its actions will do, even though contact makes the outcome uncertain and much of the scene is hidden from view. I study how robots can learn to predict the multiple possible outcomes of an action, how multiple objects move together under contact, and how a belief over occluded or unobserved object states should be updated through interaction. My long-term goal is to build world models with memory that enable long-horizon manipulation under partial observability.
